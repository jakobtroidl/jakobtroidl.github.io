I am an AI Scientist at **[HHMI Janelia](https://www.janelia.org/)**, part of the **[AI@HHMI](https://ai.hhmi.org/)** initiative, where I lead the **[MIA-AI project](https://www.hhmi.org/shaping-science/ai-hhmi#projects)**. We build large-scale datasets and pre-trained models for microscopy image analysis, using self-supervised and multi-modal learning and architectures that reason across spatial scales.

My background is in computer science and connectomics, the reconstruction and analysis of neural circuits from nanometer-resolution microscopy data. I've worked on learned models of **[neuron morphology](https://jakobtroidl.github.io/assets/pdf/neuron_shape_seasoning_preprint.pdf)** and **[self-supervised isotropic volume reconstruction](https://jakobtroidl.github.io/assets/pdf/niiv_preprint.pdf)**, proofreading and annotation infrastructure (**[CAVE](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-024-02426-z)**, **[SynAnno](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/biorxiv/early/2025/08/12/2025.08.09.669342.full.pdf)**), and tools for analyzing circuit motifs (**[Vimo](https://jakobtroidl.github.io/assets/pdf/Vimo_VIS_final_bioarxiv.pdf)**, **[MoMo](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.07.02.662847v1.full.pdf)**). I also contributed to the **[largest human connectome](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adk4858)** to date and a connectome of the **[larval zebrafish brain](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.06.10.658982v1)**.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from **[Harvard](https://www.harvard.edu/)**, advised by **[Hanspeter Pfister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanspeter_Pfister)**, with research stays in the **[Turaga Lab](https://www.janelia.org/lab/turaga-lab)** at Janelia and at **[E11.bio](https://e11.bio/)** as an ML Fellow. I hold a B.Sc. and M.Sc. in computer science from **[TU Wien](https://www.tuwien.at/en/)**.
